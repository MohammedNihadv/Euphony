## Euphony v0.2.0 — A brand-new look, and music that never stops 🎶

This is a big one. Euphony got a complete visual glow-up and a bunch of features and fixes that make everyday listening smoother.

### ✨ What's new

- **A bold new design.** Euphony now wears a striking dark theme — deep blacks, vivid colour blocks, and a clean, confident layout across every screen. Optional AMOLED "true black" for OLED phones is in Settings.
- **Music that keeps going.** Turn on *Auto-Play Similar Songs* in Settings and Euphony automatically continues with related tracks when your queue runs out — like a personal radio.
- **Artist pages that actually work.** Tap an artist to see their photo, listener count, and top songs.
- **A proper back button.** Opening an album, artist, or playlist from search now remembers where you came from, so you can get back with one tap.
- **Animated splash.** A little logo animation greets you when the app opens.
- **Clearer song details.** Track info now reads cleanly as *Artist · Album · Duration* instead of confusing labels, and the browse tiles look sharper.
- **Lock-screen & notification playback controls.**

### 🛠️ Fixes

- Fixed the app icon looking squished in the header.
- Fixed a button spilling off the edge in Settings.
- Tidied up wording throughout Settings.
- Various layout and stability polish.
