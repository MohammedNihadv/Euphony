Euphony is an open-source music streaming client for Android, built with Flutter.

Music is streamed from YouTube Music via the youtube_explode_dart library (InnerTube API). Lyrics are provided by LRCLIB (lrclib.net) and displayed as plain text.

Features:
* Ad-free music streaming from YouTube Music
* Background playback & media controls
* Offline downloads and local library support
* Lyrics display (powered by LRCLIB)
* Customizable UI themes and dynamic island
* Curated regional home feeds
* Battery and bandwidth efficient
